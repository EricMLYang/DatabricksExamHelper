## 題目內容
### 題幹
<!-- ⚠️ 保持英文原文，不要翻譯 -->
A data engineer has the following Databricks Asset Bundle (DAB) project:

```
resources:
    apps:
        bookstore_app:
            name: "bookstore_demo"
            source_code_path: ../src/bookstore
 
    volumes:
        bookstore_volume:
            name: "bookstore"
            catalog_name: demo_catalog
            schema_name: demo_schema
            grants:
                - principal: ${resources.apps.bookstore_app.id}
                privileges:
                    - READ_VOLUME
                    - WRITE_VOLUME
```

Which of the following correctly describes the result of deploying this DAB project?
### 選項
<!-- ⚠️ 保持英文原文，不要翻譯 -->
- **A.** It deploys a Volume bookstore_volume and a Service Principal bookstore_app with read and write access to the Volume.
- **B.** It generates an error because the reference `${resources.apps.bookstore_app.id}` is incorrect and should instead be `${resources.apps.bookstore_demo.id}`.
- **C.** It deploys a Catalog demo_catalog, a Schema demo_schema, a Volume bookstore_volume, and a Databricks App bookstore_app with access to the volume using a 3-level namespace.
- **D.** It deploys a Databricks App bookstore_app and a Volume bookstore_volume, and grants the Service Principal associated with the Databricks App read and write access to the Volume.

# 題目解析

---

## 📍 考點識別
**核心技術:** Databricks Asset Bundles (DABs) 資源與授權
**關鍵概念:** App 會對應 Service Principal、grants 授權到 volume
**題目關鍵字：**
- **resources.apps**: Databricks App
- **grants**: 權限授權
- **principal**: 使用 app 的 service principal

---

## ✅ 正解說明
### 為什麼 D 是正確答案？
部署後會建立 **Databricks App** 與 **Volume**。`principal: ${resources.apps.bookstore_app.id}` 會解析為該 App 的識別，並將 `READ_VOLUME`/`WRITE_VOLUME` 授權給 App 的 service principal。題目沒有定義 catalog/schema 的建立，因此不會自動建立這些物件。

---

## ❌ 錯誤選項排除
### A
**為什麼錯誤？**
- DABs 定義的是 app，不是直接建立一個名為 bookstore_app 的 service principal。

### B
**為什麼錯誤？**
- `bookstore_app` 是資源 key，參照 `${resources.apps.bookstore_app.id}` 正確。

### C
**為什麼錯誤？**
- 範例並未建立 catalog/schema，只是引用既有的 `demo_catalog` 與 `demo_schema`。

---

## 🧠 記憶法
### 口訣
**「App 建起來，權限給它的 SP。」**

### 比較表
| 資源 | 是否建立 | 說明 |
|---|---|---|
| Databricks App | ✅ | `resources.apps` 定義 |
| Volume | ✅ | `resources.volumes` 定義 |
| Catalog / Schema | ❌ | 僅引用名稱 |
